# Work and findings

The [backlog](backlog_matrix.md) owns accepted outcomes, priorities, status and
acceptance, including the remaining cluster verification criteria. Original CV
identifiers remain aliases for those criteria; retiring campaign documents does
not complete their pending verification.

Use the subject catalogs to find distinct recorded issues and proposals:

- [Execution and runtime](execution-runtime-findings.md): submission, lifecycle,
  cancellation, monitoring, qualification and recovery.
- [Scientific processing and reporting](scientific-reporting-findings.md):
  inputs, scientific stages, independent validation, Results and publication.
- [Contracts and engineering](contracts-engineering-findings.md): schemas,
  extensions, packaging, development checks, documentation and maintenance.

These catalogs combine overlapping findings while retaining original identifiers,
source evidence and dismissal reasons. They distinguish observed defects, evidence
gaps, decisions and unselected improvements; they do not create another accepted
backlog. The [five completed audits](backlog_matrix.md#october-1-2026-owner-completion)
remain complete without implying that every finding was resolved.

Focused decision briefs support [schema](schema_01_contract_audit.md),
[collaborator Analysis](extension-01-discovery.md), [release](release-readiness.md),
[cleanup](cleanup-01-ownership-audit.md), [guided operation](interactive-01-discovery.md)
and [supplied STAR index](fut_index_01_plan.md) decisions. Their matrix rows own
status and selection. Exact behavior, recovery and retained validation observations
stay beside the relevant implementation or test owner; Git retains superseded
planning and investigation transcripts.

Select an accepted item or explicitly approved objective and follow the
[development workflow](../operations/WORKFLOW.md). Listing a finding grants no
implementation, publication, cluster, artifact-deletion or evidence-promotion
authority.
