# LIB-02I — Extract the Step 09c review-package contract

## Objective

Extract the existing public thirteen-file Step `09c` review-package contract
into its permanent neutral owner and cut over every repository-owned Python
consumer without changing accepted/rejected inputs, state transitions,
exceptions/messages, generated bytes, or scientific/runtime behavior.

## Why this exists

Completed `LIB-02G` and `LIB-02H` moved the public Step `08` and Step `09`
contracts into their permanent neutral owners. Step `09c`, artifact indexing,
and reporting still obtain the public review-package roster, headers,
vocabularies, and state reduction from the private Step `09c` evidence owner.
The bottom-up boundary fixed by `LIB-02F` requires that public contract to move
before reporting can remove its remaining private-owner dependency and occupy
its final source home.

## Fixed decisions

- Target `src/norad/contracts/scientific_evidence/review_package.py` with
  mirrored direct tests at
  `tests/contracts/scientific_evidence/test_review_package.py`.
- The neutral owner is standard-library-only and uses one exact-file,
  ready-marked module identity across consumers. It does not import Step `08`,
  Step `09`, Step `09c`, or another application owner, expose compatibility
  aliases, or require a package import.
- The bounded public surface is the published science, evidence, orientation,
  implementation, validation, decision, rerun, concordance, annotation,
  adjudication, and audit vocabularies; the thirteen output headers and file
  roster; category/header/order and input-artifact bindings; the composed
  review-summary header; and `aggregate_evidence_status`.
- Step `09c` retains evidence-input and review policy, including
  `EVIDENCE_MANIFEST_HEADER`, `COMPUTATIONAL_VALIDATION_HEADER`, computational
  scope/status mappings, lower-owner aliases/utilities, `Artifact`,
  `ReviewContext`, all input and payload validation, `make_review_summary`,
  `build_context`, stable-input checks, publication, locking, rollback, and
  recovery.
- Artifact indexing consumes the neutral public contract and its existing Step
  `08` utility owner, eliminates its private Step `09c` loader, and retains its
  independently implemented reconciliation. Run-summary science cuts only its
  public roster/header/vocabulary/state-reducer references to the neutral
  owner and retains the private Step `09c` context/policy edge until a separate
  reporting-local reader slice.
- Preserve all current filenames, header and category order, vocabularies,
  accepted/rejected inputs, state transitions, exceptions/messages,
  deterministic bytes, consumer-visible module identity, and arbitrary-CWD
  behavior.
- Do not implement the reporting-local committed-package reader, move reporting
  assets, or introduce packaging, installation metadata, `PYTHONPATH`
  mutation, a wrapper, or a permanent compatibility owner.
