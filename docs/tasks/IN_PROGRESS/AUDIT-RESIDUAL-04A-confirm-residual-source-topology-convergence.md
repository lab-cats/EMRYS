# AUDIT-RESIDUAL-04A — Confirm residual source-topology convergence

## Objective

Prove that the bounded residual source-topology campaign is internally
complete at its fixed 87-path current roster and record an honest terminal
disposition for every path without expanding into the broader refactor backlog.

## Why this exists

All executable `MOVE` packages and both dedicated legacy-root reviews are
complete, but the campaign still needs one final cross-owner reconciliation.
That reconciliation must prove that final owners exist, obsolete current paths
and compatibility surfaces are absent, current callers use final paths, and
every retained, deferred, or separately retired root remains intentional. The
older `AUDIT-99` card covers a much broader modernization and documentation
backlog and cannot substitute for this bounded close.

## Fixed decisions

- Semantic planning category: `bounded documentation or low-risk maintenance`.
- Validation impact: `documentation-only/non-consuming`.
- Audit only the exact 87-path current residual roster owned by
  `FUNCTIONAL_OWNER_INVENTORY.md`, its completed `MOVE` destinations, and the
  exact final homes owned by `SOURCE_TOPOLOGY.md`.
- Treat `MOVE`, `RETAIN_ROOT`, `DEFER`, and `RETIRE` as terminal planning
  dispositions. This audit does not reopen a completed move or authorize work
  in a retained, deferred, or separately owned cleanup domain.
- Distinguish current operational callers and ownership claims from immutable
  completed cards and dated history. Historical old-path evidence is not a
  live compatibility surface.
- The strongest allowed conclusion is local/static source-topology convergence.
  It is not proof that the full modernization backlog, runtime, cluster,
  production, scientific review, or biological validation is complete.
- Do not change, move, wrap, delete, execute, or retest a product source, test,
  fixture, configuration, schema, dependency, report template, Make target, or
  harness surface.
