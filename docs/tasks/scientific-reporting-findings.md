# Scientific and reporting findings

These are retained findings and decisions, not an accepted-work queue. The
[findings matrix](backlog_matrix.md) alone owns accepted outcomes, priorities,
status and completion. Its October 1 owner disposition closed the five audit
workstreams without claiming every defect fixed or acceptance target proved.

A row distinguishes observed behavior or defect, an evidence gap, a policy
question, a reduction option, or a historical disposition. Unless the row names
an integrated repair or this documentation transfer, its current source behavior
was **not reverified**: the claim is bounded to the linked investigation. A
factual defect may be retained without an accepted repair. A proposed action is
not authorization, and a passing test is not proof of its unexercised branches.

The source corpus is frozen at [06f88dbc](https://github.com/lab-cats/EMRYS/blob/06f88dbca7161599d7445f8cb2bfedf58f377282/docs/tasks/backlog_matrix.md#L65).
ASSURANCE observations principally concern PR302 `42d02c5a`; REDUCE, DOCS and
SIZE include their own later checkpoints, recorded at each origin. This filing
revision is not a new whole-repository audit. Links on aliases preserve the full
trigger, source/caller trace, reproductions, reversals and historical evidence.
`A01-*`, `REDUCE-F*` and `DOCS-F*` retain all 818 original finding identities;
a compound original can occur in more than one clause. `POLISH:P*` and `OPT:O*`
retain 57 proposal aliases; `POLISH:A*` retains five unselected architecture
options. Their accepted-owner routes do not create additional tasks. `SIZE:*` labels name
frozen source passages, not new accepted task IDs.

Similar ownership alone is not duplication. Merged rows concern the same issue
and boundary; source-specific qualifications remain. Local source/probe or
fixture evidence does not become hosted, institutional, production, scientific
review or biological proof. CMH-ranked candidates and mechanical orientation
labels retain their existing limits. No hypothetical line estimate is a booked
saving; no protection, retained evidence, scientific policy, dependency or
supported workflow is retired by listing an option here.
